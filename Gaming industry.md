## Author

## Overview

This repository contains a Python-based data analysis project focused on understanding sales trends and patterns within the video game industry. Using a dataset (either synthetic or a real CSV), the project performs data loading, exploration, cleaning, basic statistical analysis, and generates various visualizations to provide insights into game sales across different dimensions like genre, platform, publisher, year of release, and geographical regions.

The goal is to identify key drivers of success, popular categories, and market dynamics that shape the global gaming landscape.
